---
name: Policies and procedures product rules
description: Confirmed distinctions between category-based policies and reusable procedures.
---

Policies use a shared nested subject tree independent of templates. They are grouped by category, not a sequence, and never have an execution option.

Procedures keep their steps in sequential order in the source document. Running one copies those steps to an independent project; cards may be worked on or completed in any order. Only editors and administrators may execute procedures.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguished category grouping from procedural sequence and confirmed that procedure runs must not bypass the restriction on ordinary users creating projects.

**How to apply:** Keep policy organization separate from document layouts. Do not introduce execution for policies or prerequisite gating for generated cards. Native mobile authoring is not part of the initial web feature.
