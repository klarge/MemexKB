---
name: Content list display policy
description: Default entry limits and progressive loading across content areas.
---

Content areas should show at most 20 entries initially, then provide a "Load more..." button at the bottom.

**Why:** The user requested an upper limit for the default display across content areas, naming Knowledge, Logs, Tasks, and Projects.

**How to apply:** Use twenty-entry batches in content listings, append rather than replace previously displayed entries, and retain access to the remaining entries. Apply this policy to new content areas as well.
